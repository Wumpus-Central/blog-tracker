</div><h1 id="h_01GCHTHA4SW6YGTQ47XVVMPT8P"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What email notifications can I receive from Discord?</span></h1><p><span style="color: #2e3338;">Enabling email notifications is a great way to keep track of all the shenanigans while you’re away from Discord. For instance, Discord will send transactional emails to notify users when subscription updates and purchases have been made to their account.  You cannot unsubscribe from transactional emails as these are necessary to relay important information relating to your account.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Aside from these transactional emails, other Discord emails can come in 5 different forms:</span></p><p>1. <strong>Communication Emails </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">show the calls and messages that you have missed</span></p><p>2.<strong> Social Emails </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">show pending friend requests, friend suggestions, and upcoming server events</span></p><p>3. <strong>Announcements &amp; Updates Emails </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">share policy updates, product updates, new features, and bug fixes</span></p><p>4. <strong>Tip Emails </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">provide advice on how to navigate and get the most out of Discord</span></p><p>5. <strong>Recommendation Emails </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">suggest servers and events that you might be interested in joining</span></p><h1 id="h_01GCHTHKSJ5FKEGX92BFAKV5S6"><span style="font-weight: 400;">How can I manage my marketing email notification settings?</span></h1><p>You can manage your marketing email settings in-app on PC or your browser. Note: Discord email notifications cannot be managed via mobile app. </p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">1.  Navigate to <strong>User Settings </strong>by selecting on the <strong>cogwheel</strong> [<img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/4408811448599" alt="Vector.png">] located on the bottom left corner.</span></p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img class="wysiwyg-image-resized" style="aspect-ratio: 750/132; width: 58.29%;" src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44480746305431" alt="Cogwheel Icon" width="750" height="132"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M5MNNKPF9DT2XWXJ4SQDHGK2"><span style="color: #666666;">Cogwheel Icon</span></h4><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">2. Afterwards, press on the <strong>Notifications </strong>tab and scroll down to the <strong>Email</strong> section. From here, you can see more information about each type of notification and select the ones you want to receive.</span></p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44480716957591" alt="Email Settings in Notifications" width="2426" height="1750"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M5MNSCTH5V4G5VR71ATZVJVH"><span style="color: #666666;">Email Settings in Notifications</span></h4><p><span style="color: #2e3338;">3. Customizing which marketing emails you’d like to receive is a great way to declutter your space while still being part of the loop! If you would like to unsubscribe from all marketing emails, you can simply press <strong>Unsubscribe</strong> at the bottom of the <strong>Email</strong> section.</span></p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44480716958743" alt="Unsubscribe Button in Email Settings" width="2280" height="1154"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M5MP1D97Q4KFVR7AMYZYE3TM"><span style="color: #666666;">Unsubscribe Button in Email Settings</span></h4><h1 id="h_01M5MP6DMJB3BBT1R280J19STM"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Can I unsubscribe from marketing email notifications within the emails?</span></h1><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You can unsubscribe from any or all marketing emails at any time by pressing Unsubscribe in the email itself.</span></p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><span style="color: #2e3338;"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44480716959255" alt="Unsubscribe within Email" width="624" height="332"></span></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M5MP8JYBRSAKD2M614RGDJP5"><span style="color: #666666;">Unsubscribe within Email</span></h4><div class="warn-box">
<span style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Important</strong>: </span>If you choose to unsubscribe in the email itself, you will no longer receive marketing emails. <span style="color: #2e3338;">You can change your settings if you want to receive marketing emails again.</span>
</div><h1 id="h_01M5MPBAN72QE843KXRG88RXNE">Can I disable email tracking?</h1><p>Discord uses email pixels to track analytics relating to emails that we send.  We track when emails are opened to learn what is useful (and what is not).  We maintain tracking for transactional emails for deliverability, security, and legal purposes where appropriate. To disable email tracking of marketing emails, you can unsubscribe from all marketing emails.  </p>

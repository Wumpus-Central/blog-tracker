<p>Discord is designed for real-time messaging, but we do offer personalized experiences that help you get back into conversations and find features you may like. You can choose whether to have a personalized experience in your Settings. If you have personalization turned on, we will use information about you to rank or select Discord-curated experiences. This article provides more information on how these features (sometimes called "automated systems") work.</p><div class="toc-box">

</div><h1 id="h_personalization_shop">Shop</h1><p>We want you to find the avatars, decorations, profile effects, and more that help you customize your Discord experience. To do that, we look at things like your previous purchases, the games you play, the servers you are in, and other account activity to tailor your Shop experience.</p><h1 id="h_personalization_other_features">Other Features</h1><p>For other features, like <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/36415877391511-Discord-Checkpoint-2025" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Checkpoint</a> and <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/22045487931799-Members-List-Recent-Activity-FAQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Activity Cards</a>, we deliver relevant content to you such as highlighting information from your top servers or activity from your close friends using parameters like:</p><ul>
<li data-list-item-id="e9a96e025dc731e8c5e9cd35ce3cb0447">The games you play and how you play</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e3440e6bc556dd219885450ba84cd7657">The servers you are in and how you interact in them</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e9cff677405bd235e467ba3a6ffb02b72">How you interact with your friends and other users</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e84b076782eb38b83b92894a015e5f369">The number of messages you send</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e1d71791a1c771d4283c1ad34c54d2a9b">The time you spend in voice/video calls</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e4acdee82c180862011ab2600e64061a5">Whether you are a Nitro member and which plan you are on</li>
</ul><p>We do not use your individual message contents or access your voice/video calls. You can control this use of your data with the product personalization setting, <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience</strong>, in your <strong>Data &amp; Privacy</strong> User settings. <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/21865322754327-Data-Used-to-Personalize-Discord" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learn more</a>.</p>

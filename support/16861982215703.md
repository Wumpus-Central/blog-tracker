<li data-list-item-id="e08a1fa0c5caf0658a224af591e38dd92">

In these games, Auto Clips can work even when you're not in a voice channel.</li>

</div><h1 id="h_in-control">You’re in Control</h1><p>Clips and Auto Clips are built so you decide what gets saved and shared:</p><ul>
<li data-list-item-id="e8cc12acea85e82faf2c7ca69b9f5724e">Your Clips don't reach our servers unless you choose to share them.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="eabf60ae59ff2d4239f0cf740ccb90546">You'll always see a clear indicator when you're in a call with Clips enabled.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e18764ea90966653bfac908459bf07820">At any time, you can turn off Auto Clips, disable clipping on your account entirely, and even opt out of having your voice included in others' Clips altogether.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e660e6052effce17858a2ba4598b2c4c7">Teens' voices are left out of other people's Clips unless they choose otherwise.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="ea334f69b4599cbb7b24e99571baae40b">We do not train the model on your Clips, your voice, or user data. Instead, we built Auto Clips in direct collaboration with streamers and creators.</li>
</ul><p>If someone shares a Clip of you that breaks our Community Guidelines, you can <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/22582288274071-Reporting-Abusive-Behavior-to-Discord" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">report it</a> the same way you'd report any other message.</p><h1 id="h_faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h1><p><strong>Q: When do Clips record?</strong></p><p>A: While you play, Auto Clips runs in the background and temporarily captures your last 60 seconds of game and voice, locally on your computer, so you can save that Clip. Anything that isn’t saved as a Clip is discarded when your session ends. You'll always see a clear indicator when you're in a call with Clips enabled.</p><p><strong>Q: Does Discord use my clips to train Auto Clips?</strong></p><p>A: No, we don't train on your Clips. Your Clips also don't reach our servers unless you choose to share them.</p><p><strong>Q: What data does Auto Clips read from my games?</strong></p><p>A: Auto Clips looks for two kinds of signals while you play, both processed on your computer:</p><ul>
<li data-list-item-id="e28ec57602e23be03b3179adfca045c16">
<strong>Voice:</strong> When you’re in a voice channel, a small machine learning model listens for audio cues resembling laughter and shouting. It can’t understand words or tell who is speaking.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e52eddab8355dda22c5e4af8eeed149c0">
<strong>Game events:</strong> In supported games, Auto Clips uses the available game integrations to spot moments like a kill, a goal, or the end of a round.</li>
</ul><p>These signals tell Auto Clips when a big moment happened to help you capture a Clip.</p><p><strong>Q: Will Auto Clips slow down my computer?</strong></p><p>A: The Auto Clips detection itself is lightweight and uses less than one CPU core. Clips is only turned on by default for computers that can support it. If you notice any issues with your game's performance, you can turn off Clips in your settings.</p><p>That's everything you need to start saving and sharing your favorite gaming moments. Turn on Clips or Auto Clips, jump into a game, and share your best moments with friends.</p><div class="form-box">

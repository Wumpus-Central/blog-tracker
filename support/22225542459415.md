<p>Sponsored content is paid for by our partners, such as game developers, who want to offer our users a reward for engaging with their game or related content via <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/22225719947543" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Quests</a> while using Discord.</p><p>We may tell you about sponsored content that we think will interest you based on your use of Discord and your activities on the services that are associated with your Discord account, as well as data about you that we may receive from advertisers and third-party data providers.  </p><p>If you’re not interested in receiving personalized sponsored content based on your use of Discord and your activities on the services that are associated with your Discord account, you can control the use of this data.  </p><p>If you’re not interested in receiving personalized sponsored content based on data about you that we may receive from advertisers and third-party data providers—including how you engage with games and other content or services outside of Discord—you can also opt out of this use of your data.</p><h1 id="h_01K39NVEFJEHMV91CTJV4BA49G">Desktop/Browser </h1><p>1. On the Discord desktop app or browser client, go to <strong>User Settings &gt; Data &amp; Privacy &gt; </strong><span style="color: #2e3338;"><strong>Sponsored Content</strong></span>. </p><p>2. Configure these options:</p><ul>
<li class="wysiwyg-list-color" style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338;" data-list-item-id="e9a264c5d191c429c218c27584c13a219"><span style="color: #2e3338;"><strong>Use my Discord activity to personalize Sponsored Content</strong>: Uses your Discord activity, such as the games you play, to surface relevant sponsored content.</span></li>
<li class="wysiwyg-list-color" style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338;" data-list-item-id="e480ce86171d779428794499188119a51"><span style="color: #2e3338;"><strong>Use third-party data to personalize sponsored content</strong>: Uses your data from advertisers and other third parties to surface relevant Sponsored Content.</span></li>
</ul><figure class="wysiwyg-image"><img style="aspect-ratio: 800/575;" src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44429851978775" alt="Data &amp; Privacy controls in Data &amp; Privacy on desktop" width="800" height="575"></figure><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01K39NWSZR8W47G1T1PD4342S1"><span style="color: #666666;">Data &amp; Privacy controls in Data &amp; Privacy on desktop</span></h4><h1 id="h_01K39NX8PZP3HVHVEYMZ5W5T9X">Mobile </h1><p>1. Adjust the <strong>Use my Discord activity to personalize Sponsored Content </strong>and the<strong> Use third-party data to personalize Sponsored Content </strong>options to your preference. Learn more about these options as described <a href="#h_01K39NVEFJEHMV91CTJV4BA49G">above</a>.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img class="wysiwyg-image-resized" style="aspect-ratio: 720/1496; width: 42%;" src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44429849325463" alt="Data &amp; Privacy controls in Data &amp; Privacy on mobile" width="720" height="1496"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01K5EQFH9JCE05ESYVBYYKKYKE"><span style="color: #666666;">Data &amp; Privacy controls in Data &amp; Privacy on mobile</span></h4><div class="info-box">

<ul>

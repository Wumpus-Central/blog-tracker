<p>We use certain information to help us understand and improve Discord and to develop features, systems, and models that make Discord safer and better for you to use. This includes information such as when and how you use our services, which features you interact with, the games you play, the things you search for, and how servers are structured. It can also include public message content in Discoverable servers.</p><p>Turning the <strong>Use Data to Improve Discord </strong>setting off means we will no longer use new information about how you use and/or navigate Discord to understand and improve our products. We may continue to use your data to make Discord safer; we may also continue to use the information we collected when the setting was enabled. More detail can be found in our <a href="https://discord.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Privacy Policy</a>. </p><p>On desktop, head to <strong>User Setting &gt; Data &amp; Privacy</strong> and on mobile, head to <strong>Settings &gt; Data &amp; Privacy</strong> to disable the <strong>Use Data to Improve Discord </strong>toggle.<strong> </strong></p><figure class="wysiwyg-image"><img style="aspect-ratio: 2264/1184;" src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44429871688983" alt="Use data to improve Discord toggle in Data &amp; Privacy on Desktop" width="2264" height="1184"></figure><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01K488Z9QAV1HA3YCK74AFZCFQ"><span style="color: #666666;">Use data to improve Discord toggle in Data &amp; Privacy on Desktop</span></h4><p>It will also prevent us from automatically improving the quality of voice or video communications on Discord based on your usage.</p><p>If you’d like to further assert your right to object to our processing of your data, email <a href="mailto:privacy@discord.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>privacy@discord.com</strong></a>.</p><div class="info-box">When you turn the setting off, the signals associated with your use of Discord continue to be sent, but we do not use the information for the purposes described above. This is an account-based setting and applies to your use of Discord regardless of the device used to access your account. This allows us to keep your account experience consistent across devices and access points of Discord.</div>

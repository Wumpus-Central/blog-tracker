</div><h1 id="h_01J7XTC8Q6SW90XDWF1XATHXDB">What is a Custom Status?</h1><p>Do you ever wish you could tell your Discord friends you're busy battling dragons or waiting for your sourdough to rise? <strong>Custom status</strong> is a feature that lets your creativity rise higher than perfectly proofed bread dough. Share your mood, activity, or a witty one-liner, making your presence as unique as your secret family recipe.</p><p>Ready to spice up your Discord profile? Grab your favorite emoji (🍞 anyone?) and let's dive in!</p><h1 id="h_01J7XVFDVZNMAQ10B0K5SJFYN9">Setting a Custom Status</h1><h2 id="h_01J7XTE8GQX8Q0M2YMP0DMF596">Desktop/Browser</h2><p>1. On the desktop or browser app, select your <strong>profile</strong> icon in the lower-left corner of the app to view your <strong>profile</strong> menu.</p><p>2. Select the <strong>Add Status</strong> bubble to create a <strong>custom status</strong>.</p><div class="tip-box">
<strong>Tip:</strong> Select the emoji icon to set an emoji to your custom status. With <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/115000435108-What-are-Nitro-Nitro-Basic" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Discord Nitro or Nitro Basic</a>, you can use custom server emojis too!</div><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110091012631" alt="Add Status bubble in the profile menu on desktop" width="600" height="508"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH9GAKZ24KZTP9B0VHW"><span style="color: #666666;"><strong>Add Status</strong> Bubble in the Profile Menu on Desktop</span></h4><p>3. To manage when your custom status clears, select an option from the <strong>Clear After</strong> pull-down menu.</p><p>4. To choose what status you would like to set, select an option from the <strong>Status</strong> pull-down menu.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110091014807" alt="Set a custom status menu with the Clear After and Status pull-down menus on desktop" width="456" height="504"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH94107ABA3ZX81AJ1X"><span style="color: #666666;"><strong>Clear After</strong> and <strong>Status</strong> Pull-Down Menus on Desktop</span></h4><p>5. When your custom status is all set, select <strong>Save</strong> in the lower-right corner of the <strong>Set a custom status</strong> menu.</p><div class="info-box">
<strong>Note:</strong> Custom statuses expire after one day by default, but you can configure them to last for shorter or longer periods.</div><h2 id="h_01J7XTMQVECS5VQJNQ5XS1DW9K">Mobile</h2><p>1. On the mobile app, long-press your <strong>profile</strong> icon in the lower-right corner of the app to view your <strong>profile</strong> menu in <strong>Account</strong>. You can also tap your <strong>profile</strong> icon &gt; <strong>Add Status</strong>.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134514583" alt="Profile menu in Account on mobile" width="285" height="590"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH92YJ7XFH2E6Z92DG2"><span style="color: #666666;">Profile Menu in <strong>Account</strong> on Mobile</span></h4><p>2. Tap <strong>Set a custom status</strong> to create a custom status.</p><p>3. In <strong>Edit Status</strong>, create your custom status.</p><p>4. Choose from the <strong>Status Duration</strong> menu to set when your status will automatically clear.</p><p>5. Once your custom status is ready, tap <strong>Save</strong> in the upper-right corner to confirm. This will lead you back to <strong>Account</strong>.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134528151" alt="Edit Status screen on mobile" width="284" height="592">  <img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134529815" alt="Status Duration menu on mobile" width="285" height="592"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH9HM50FBF9ZV2SKKPH"><span style="color: #666666;">Left: <strong>Edit Status</strong> Screen on Mobile</span></h4><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH983JJ18RQ5RASRXZ0"><span style="color: #666666;">Right: <strong>Status Duration</strong> Menu on Mobile</span></h4><p>6. In <strong>Account</strong>, you can also manage your profile status by selecting an option from the <strong>Online Status</strong> menu.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134531863" alt="Online Status menu in Account on mobile" width="302" height="628"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH9JDN82W6MRK3ZSQEZ"><span style="color: #666666;"><strong>Online Status</strong> Menu in <strong>Account</strong> on Mobile</span></h4><h1 id="h_01J7XVH0E2H42XQ04NG5FFJ22Y">Editing or Clearing a Custom Status</h1><h2 id="h_01J7XTSMP2Y99M6692SBZKCCT7">Desktop/Browser</h2><p>1. To edit or clear your custom status on desktop or browser, select your <strong>profile</strong> icon in the lower-left corner of your app.</p><p>2. Select your custom status.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110091017111" alt="Current custom status in the profile menu on desktop" width="245" height="53"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M878AAH9PMBZB86V9YK1P337"><span style="color: #666666;">Current Custom Status in the Profile Menu on Desktop</span></h4><p>3. From here, you can:</p><ul>
<li data-list-item-id="ee14b10bdf0afe484eb4abad453172dd3">
<strong>To clear your custom status</strong>, select the <strong>trash</strong> icon [<img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134534167" alt="Trash icon" width="24" height="24">] to remove it.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e172cebff7505e872c0222d627a60c0a3">
<strong>To edit your custom status</strong>, select the <strong>pencil</strong> icon [<img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134535319" alt="Pencil icon" width="24" height="24">] to make changes.</li>
</ul><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134536855" alt="Trash icon used to clear a custom status on desktop" width="274" height="552">  <img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134538263" alt="Pencil icon used to edit a custom status on desktop" width="273" height="550"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01J7XTTHET5PXJQ0Q24527MSWX"><span style="color: #666666;">Left: Trash Icon to Clear a Custom Status</span></h4><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01J7XTTHETJC9MZRC8NYXF0Y7A"><span style="color: #666666;">Right: Pencil Icon to Edit a Custom Status</span></h4><div class="info-box">
<strong>Note:</strong> Custom statuses override other activity statuses in a server's <strong>members list</strong> and <strong>friends list</strong> within <strong>direct messages</strong> (<strong>DMs</strong>) and <strong>group chats</strong>. However, activity statuses will still show when viewing a user's <strong>full profile</strong>.</div><h2 id="h_01J7XTXXKQERWK3GA1BSE8Q77B">Mobile</h2><p>1. From here, you can:</p><ul>
<li data-list-item-id="e7afed71905d4ffd13f29533fde476040">
<strong>To clear your custom status</strong>, tap the <strong>x</strong> icon [<img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110091023639" alt="X icon" width="24" height="24">] to the right of your custom status to remove it.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e50e379eaa7aeb92b61b4ee8b58f72fbb">
<strong>To edit your custom status</strong>, tap your current <strong>custom status</strong> to make changes.</li>
</ul><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134539671" alt="X icon used to clear a custom status on mobile" width="294" height="609">  <img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110091031319" alt="Editing a current custom status on mobile" width="293" height="606"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01J7XTZK6FD0SVZB4SBWRJCZQE"><span style="color: #666666;">Left: X Icon to Clear a Custom Status</span></h4><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01J7XTZK6FZRWSQG6K9V6WX3NV"><span style="color: #666666;">Right: Current Custom Status to Edit</span></h4><h1 id="h_sharing-custom-status-updates">Sharing Your Custom Status Updates</h1><div class="exp-box">
<strong>Important:</strong> The <strong>Share when I update my custom status</strong> setting is currently an experiment and is not widely available at this time.</div><p>When you update your custom status, the friends you talk to most may get a notification about it on their mobile devices. If you'd rather not notify your friends, you can turn this off with the <strong>Share when I update my custom status</strong> setting.</p><p>This setting is on by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it off.</p><div class="info-box">
<strong>Note:</strong> This setting is saved to your account, so turning it off on one device turns it off on all your devices. It only stops the notification to your friends. It doesn't change who can see your custom status.</div><h2 id="h_sharing-desktop-browser">Desktop/Browser</h2><p>1. Select the <strong>cogwheel</strong> next to your username to open your <strong>User Settings</strong>.</p><p>2. Under <strong>Games &amp; Apps</strong>, select <strong>Activity Privacy</strong>.</p><p>3. Under <strong>What Activity I Share</strong>, find <strong>Share when I update my custom status</strong>, directly below <strong>Share when I come online</strong>.</p><p>4. Toggle the setting off to stop notifying friends when you update your custom status. You can turn it back on at any time.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134541335" alt="Share when I update my custom status toggle under What Activity I Share in Activity Privacy on desktop" width="624" height="325"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M7NKMXEB85T5WD9MCVH42F00"><span style="color: #666666;"><strong>Share when I update my custom status</strong> in <strong>Activity Privacy</strong> on Desktop</span></h4><h2 id="h_01M7NKMXEBDF7J8BDG42DM8N5H">Mobile</h2><p>1. Tap your avatar in the bottom-right corner of the app.</p><p>2. Tap the <strong>cogwheel</strong> to open <strong>Settings</strong>.</p><p>3. Tap <strong>Content &amp; Social</strong>.</p><p>4. Under <strong>Discord</strong>, find <strong>Share when I update my custom status</strong>, directly below <strong>Share when I come online</strong>.</p><p>5. Toggle the setting off to stop notifying friends when you update your custom status. You can turn it back on at any time.</p><p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center"><img src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/45110134541719" alt="Share when I update my custom status toggle under Discord in Content and Social settings on mobile" width="285" height="618"></p><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M7NKMXEB4VTJK1FN7V3KEZRP"><span style="color: #666666;"><strong>Share when I update my custom status</strong> in <strong>Content &amp; Social</strong> on Mobile</span></h4><div class="info-box">
<strong>Note:</strong> Want to stop getting these notifications from your friends instead? On the mobile app, go to <strong>Settings &gt; Notifications</strong> and turn off <strong>Status Notifications</strong>. This setting is only available on mobile.</div><div class="book-box">

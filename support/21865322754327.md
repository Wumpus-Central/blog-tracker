<p><span style="color: #2e3338;">Discord uses certain information to </span><span style="color: #23272a;">personalize your experience and our services. When the “<strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience</strong>” setting is enabled, we tailor features to help you discover relevant content, highlight activity you care about, and surface features and promotions from us and our partners that may interest you. </span></p><div class="toc-box">

</div><h1 id="h_01M5DQKMX3FF1VJTZ34HHHKGB3"><span style="color: #2e3338;">How Personalization Works</span></h1><p><span style="color: #2e3338;">When personalization is turned on, Discord uses various activity signals to customize your experience, such as:</span></p><ul>
<li data-list-item-id="e61ed39b6b4ad6c39b504118f1ffd6b83">
<strong>Activity Feeds &amp; Sidebars</strong>: Friends and users you interact with frequently appear higher in the “Active Now” sidebar and Friends tab feed.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e7a7ebf2f31e19ff70eea970ed2c1cef1">
<strong>Recommendations &amp; Discovery</strong>: Personalized shop offerings in the Collectibles Shop, and suggested content based on your interests.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="e8719adcd503a1b7c2023db2a57d07a9d">
<strong>Smart</strong> <strong>Sorting</strong>: Smarter default suggestions when starting group DMs or joining voice channels.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="eed2f3868c1161c990e434906572afaec">
<strong>Profile Summaries &amp; Gaming Badges</strong>: Personalized activity summaries (like Checkpoint recap cards) and progress tracking toward earned gaming badges.</li>
<li data-list-item-id="ef29572c76a5a53cf22e2675122f18a95">
<strong>Relevant Notifications &amp; Promotions</strong>: Activity reminders about friends playing games, server updates, and relevant product or gifting suggestions.</li>
</ul><h1 id="h_01M5DQNNE68D708JGEYWFDRV7V"><span style="color: #2e3338;">What Data is Used</span></h1><p><span style="color: #2e3338;">To power these features, we use information about the way you use Discord like: </span></p><ul>
<li class="wysiwyg-list-color" style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338;" data-list-item-id="ecae01cfe6874627b8e52583c1e4606bc"><span style="color: #2e3338;">Games you play and activity on Discord</span></li>
<li class="wysiwyg-list-color" style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338;" data-list-item-id="e1f32ac1783031e6ba4cc21cc0f76fb2f"><span style="color: #2e3338;">Who you interact with most frequently</span></li>
<li class="wysiwyg-list-color" style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338;" data-list-item-id="ed1eebeeb05ef107d1f66bfd9a1c1dcb7"><span style="color: #2e3338;">Servers you join and participate in</span></li>
<li class="wysiwyg-list-color" style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338;" data-list-item-id="ef8b86fcb73dc4de98c9a2ce39e453da9"><span style="color: #2e3338;">Connections you make between your Discord account and another service (e.g., Xbox or Steam)</span></li>
</ul><h1 id="h_01M5DQQ1R6XPBJD5FTB2SQPVRF"><span style="color: #2e3338;">How to Control Personalization</span></h1><p>Depending on your age or region, your default setting may be different. To control personalization on desktop, head to <strong>User Setting &gt; Data &amp; Privacy</strong>, and on mobile, head to <strong>Settings &gt; Data &amp; Privacy</strong> to change the <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience </strong>toggle.</p><figure class="wysiwyg-image"><img style="aspect-ratio: 2271/1024;" src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44429897320215" alt="Use data to personalize my Discord experience toggle in Data &amp; Privacy on Desktop" width="2271" height="1024"></figure><h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01K4888RH0XM1CN4CJA62MMJ9R"><span style="color: #2e3338;">Use data to personalize my Discord experience toggle in Data &amp; Privacy on Desktop</span></h4><p><span style="color: #2e3338;">Disabling the <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience </strong>toggle means we will no longer use your personal information to personalize features or recommend content to you.</span></p><p><span style="color: #2e3338;">Your ability to access conversations, voice channels, or other spaces on Discord will not be impacted by this setting.</span></p>

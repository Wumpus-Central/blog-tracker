Some are small spaces for your closest friends, while others are BIG and have hundreds, if not thousands, of members. </p><p><strong>In this blog, we’ll be organizing Role permissions by three recommended categories</strong>: </p><ul role="list"><li>Role permissions for all members </li><li>Role permissions for your moderators</li><li>Role permissions for your superpowerful server admins </li></ul><p>All listed permissions are currently in-app as of June 6, 2025. </p>

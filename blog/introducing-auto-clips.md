<p>
 Growing up, video games were my haven: a place for escape, connection, and constant discovery. I still remember rushing to LAN setups after school to play Counter-Strike with my friends, clutching eco rounds, and defusing the bomb with half a second left on the timer. Those shared moments defined my youth, and I've always wished I could relive them.
</p>
<p>
 That drive is why I've dedicated my career to improving how gaming memories are captured and shared. I co-founded the gameplay clipping app Powder alongside Maryan Morel, Tom Mason, and Titouan Loeb, and the four of us now continue that mission at Discord. Discord is already where friends hang out and play every day, and we couldn't be more excited to bring clipping right into the heart of that experience. Over the past year, we’ve been working to fully revamp the clipping experience so players can effortlessly capture and share their greatest gaming moments.
</p>

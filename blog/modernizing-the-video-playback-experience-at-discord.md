<p>
 Discord is the best way to chat and game with your friends. Part of that is ensuring that it’s a great place to share all types of media as well, such as pictures and video content. And that’s exactly what the Media Infrastructure team focuses on: making media awesome to share and watch on Discord!
</p>
<p>
 <strong>
  Today, we want to talk specifically about
  <em>
   videos
  </em>
  .
 </strong>
 With more than 90 million people using Discord every day, sharing videos with friends is a huge part of how people hang out here. Our Media Infrastructure team decided to step back and think deeply about how to make the video-watching experience even better.
</p>
<p>
 We set ourselves three goals:
</p>
<ul role="list">
 <li>
  <strong>
   Modernize the user experience
  </strong>
  by giving it a fresh coat of paint!
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Lay a foundation for great video playback everywhere in Discord.
  </strong>
  After all, one of our engineering mantras is “Build technology to be reusable.”
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Make it fast and safe to change.
  </strong>
  We’ve got a
  <em>
   lot
  </em>
  of great ideas planned, and we want any team to be able to improve the player without holding their breath!
 </li>
</ul>
<p>
 This is the story of how we assessed and refactored two video players into one, cutting the player code each product maintained by up to 94%(!!), and ultimately swapping in the first version of the new player without any users noticing.
</p>

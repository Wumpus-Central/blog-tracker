<h4>
 <strong>
  📱 Mighty Mobile Updates
 </strong>
</h4>
<ul role="list">
 <li>
  <em>
   Tappitytap
  </em>
  -
  <strong>
   Tap to React lets you double-tap to quickly react to any message with a preset emoji
  </strong>
  . You can assign a particular emoji as your Tap to React shortcut in User Settings &gt; Chat.
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   You 🫵 raise the Bar
  </strong>
  . A new “You Bar” on mobile gives more prominence to your identity and helps simplify navigation. Tap it to view your profile, or
  <a href="https://discord.com/blog/you-bar">
   peek at our blog post about it.
  </a>
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Mobile users will now be asked if they
   <em>
    reeaally
   </em>
   wanted to call that DM
  </strong>
  or Group DM before ringing the entire chat at once. No more accidentally scaring the group at 3am.
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   On iOS, photos upload a bit quicker
  </strong>
  thanks to
  <strong>
  </strong>
  some compression and latency optimizations.
  <strong>
  </strong>
  We’re talkin’
  <strong>
  </strong>
  17% smaller files, and around 12% lower latency.
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Wishlists are now on mobile
  </strong>
  , letting you add Shop items to your profile’s Wishlist wherever you find yourself. You can even peek at other people’s wishlists on their profiles while going…
  <em>
   outside
  </em>
  .
 </li>
</ul>
<h4>
 <strong>
  💬 Chats, Channels, and In-Game Comms
 </strong>
</h4>
<ul role="list">
 <li>
  <strong>
   Queue up with friends easier through Discord &amp; Riot account linking!
  </strong>
  <a href="https://discord.com/blog/making-it-easier-than-ever-to-connect-with-friends-in-league-val">
   Rolling out to League of Legends and VALORANT
  </a>
  , you’ll be able to see your Discord friends list in-game and invite a Discord friend directly from the game client.
  <strong>
   ‍
  </strong>
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Pin DMs and Group DMs
  </strong>
  to keep them at the top of your DM list. You can also pin channels to the top of a server’s channel list to avoid trudging through hundreds of similarly-named channels.
  <strong>
   ‍
  </strong>
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Invites to join voice now show who's hanging out
  </strong>
  , that way you’re not accidentally jump-scaring someone streaming Resident Evil Requiem upon joining.
  <strong>
   ‍
  </strong>
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Say “👋hi uwu” to your new best friend!
  </strong>
  On desktop, after you add someone new, you’ll be encouraged to send them a friendly wave within your DM list. You can still send whatever you’d like, though.
  <strong>
   ‍
  </strong>
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Game Profile pages got an upgrade
  </strong>
  . Click a game’s name while someone’s playing to see screenshots, what platforms it’s on, Steam/OpenCritic reviews, how popular it is on Discord, a link to the game’s official server, and more.
 </li>
</ul>

<p>
 Discord and NVIDIA are working together to bring cloud-streamed gaming experiences directly to Discord, powered by the
 <a href="https://geforcenow.com">
  NVIDIA GeForce NOW cloud gaming service.
 </a>
 This new integration will transform game discovery for both players and game developers, making it easier than ever to find and try new games directly on Discord – no downloads, installs, or launchers. The cloud-streamed game experience will start with
 <em>
  Fortnite
 </em>
 , which will be demoed behind closed doors at gamescom this week.
</p>
<p>
 “Game discovery today can be clunky and time-consuming with downloads, installs, and patches. We believe collaborating with NVIDIA to power an instant play experience using GeForce NOW will transform game discovery, making it easier to jump into a game instantly without missing a beat of gameplay with friends. We see this new premium experience as a game-changer for players and a massive opportunity for developers to get their titles in front of highly engaged player communities on Discord faster than ever.” – Stanislav Vishnevskiy, Co-Founder and CTO at Discord.
</p>
<p>
 The demo is powered by the NVIDIA Graphics Delivery Network (GDN) through GeForce NOW, ensuring that players can experience
 <em>
  Fortnite
 </em>
 at up to 60 frames per second at 1440p resolution, straight from the cloud. An integration like this offers a glimpse at how players who don’t have the game installed on their PC would be able to jump into a game and play directly inside Discord, with a limited-time trial of the GeForce NOW Performance experience. Since there’s no separate launcher or installation required, players would be able to enjoy creating a fast, social, and frictionless new path to the games they love, or quickly discover their next favorite game.
</p>

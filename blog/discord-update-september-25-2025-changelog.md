<h3>
 Big Updates for Big Chillin
</h3>
<ul role="list">
 <li>
  <strong>
   User Profiles on desktop got a refreshed look!
  </strong>
  They’ve been updated to help show people more details about who you are, what you’re all about, and the games you’ve been playing lately. And if you’re doing a LOT of stuff at once? Boy, do we have even
  <em>
   better
  </em>
  news for you:
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   Playing games, listening to music, AND in a voice channel? Show ‘em ALL off!
  </strong>
  If you’re doing multiple things at once, they’ll
  <em>
  </em>
  now all show as a stack of cards on your profile.
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   On desktop, Activities can now be opened in a pop-out window
  </strong>
  . Open your Watch Together session in a floating window while you speed through your DMs and servers. No one has
  <em>
   ever
  </em>
  had the technology to watch a video while doing something else on their PC. Until
  <em>
   now
  </em>
  .
 </li>
 <li>
  <strong>
   When you turn your device’s camera on, a sound now plays
  </strong>
  to let you know that people can see your collection of Coke Zero cans, mechanical keyboards, or whatever the hell those ugly labu-things are.
 </li>
</ul>

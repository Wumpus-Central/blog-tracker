<p>This year, we&#x27;re reminded that Pride was founded as a protest in 1969, when people brave enough to live authentically in the face of dangerous adversity changed the lives of LGBTQIA+ people in the US and abroad forever. Their actions and advocacy continue to inspire us today, and while we&#x27;ve made tremendous progress over the last 56 years, work remains to ensure everyone receives the dignity, respect, and equality they deserve. In that spirit, this June, our PRIDE Employee Resource Group (ERG) came together to celebrate the meaningful act of being who you are, especially when it still takes courage to do so. </p><p>Here at Discord, we believe that your authentic self belongs here, and building belonging is at the heart of what we do.  As one of nine Employee Resource Groups at Discord open to <em>all</em> employees, we also believe that when team members bring their whole selves to work, we’re not just contributing our talents – we’re demonstrating that our collective unique perspectives create greater impact.</p>

<p>
 Back in
 <em>
  ancient times
 </em>
 (circa 2017), Discord Nitro started out as a way to support us in our early days while getting some sweet perks in the process. It had just four perks at the start, but those perks immediately became the stuff you associate with Nitro: animated avatars, custom emoji anywhere, bigger file uploads, and a snazzy profile badge.
</p>
<p>
 Eight years later, Nitro’s first four features have expanded
 <a href="https://discord.com/nitro">
  to nearly 20 in total
 </a>
 ! Now it’s got HD streaming to share your game with smoother frames, the power to use stickers and soundboard sounds anywhere, and the latest feature: Custom Themes, to help make Discord match whatever mood you’re going for on your setup. Nearly every corner of Discord gets better when you’ve got Nitro!
</p>

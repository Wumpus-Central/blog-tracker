 <strong>
  Bookmarks and Reminders
  <br/>
 </strong>
 Right click a message anywhere and you can bookmark it and come back to it - whenever you want! You’ll find it in your inbox tab, for your eyes only. You can do the same to create a reminder, which will give you a little nudge when it’s due.

 <strong>
  Scheduled Messages
  <br/>
 </strong>
 You know that little plus button you use to add attachments? You can also press that button to schedule a message for the future! Great for announcements or when you need to send a message in the morning but want to sleep in.
</p>

 <strong>
  @time is now on Mobile
  <br/>
 </strong>
 @time mentions are now on mobile! Everyone sees the time in their own timezones, so you can schedule a meeting time with your friends or server members - even when you're on the go.

 <strong>
  Pick Now, GIF Later
 </strong>
 <br/>
 Holding Shift while clicking (or long-pressing on mobile) GIFs in the GIF picker now gives you the URL to paste into your message instead of sending it immediately. Now you can add some context to your GIF before you trip up for like 35 seconds trying to write “its u haha,” ruining the WHOLE payoff. (thats u haha)
